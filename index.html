9FoamClass A · Class B · foam lines

Class A foam

Active firefighting0.1%–0.3%
Salvage / overhaul0.3%–0.5%
Exposure protection0.5%–1%

Class B foam

Hydrocarbons – 3%0.16 GPM per sq ft for 20 min
Polar solvents – 3%0.26 GPM per sq ft for 20 min

Sq ft of spill × (0.16 or 0.26) × 20 = total gallons
Foam usage = total gallons × % of foam

Foam lines

PDP = 200 PSI with eductor at the pump panel
PDP = 200 PSI + FL of hose if eductor is away from the pump panel
